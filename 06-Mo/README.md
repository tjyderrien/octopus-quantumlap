# Advancement of various tasks with Mo

## Ground state convergence

June 22nd, 2020

* Band structure was verified by comparison with materialsproject.org. 
* Fermi energy was prepared as function of electron temperature (to be verified by Y. Levy). 

## Linear response of Mo

June 22nd, 2020

* Using utility oct-dielectric of octopus, linear response provided results that are very well repeating the experimental data, even in the IR region (most complicated). This could actually make a little publication. 
* This secures the preparation of the ground state of Mo for non-equliibrium computations. 

## Time-dependent optical properties

* Before starting massive computation, we need to obtain the optical response using home-made routine. See the report on Au for more details.
* Total absorbed energy was computed as function of laser intensity. Same difficulty as for Au is to reconstruct how much laser energy is reaching the bulk of the sample. See the same problem with Au for details.
